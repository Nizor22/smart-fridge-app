Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

TL;DR: We collect only what's needed to run the app. We don't sell your data. You can delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

1. Who We Are

Smart Fridge AI ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile application that helps users track food inventory, reduce waste, and discover recipes using artificial intelligence. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — for authentication and account recovery
  • First and last name — for personalization
  • Phone number (optional) — if you choose to provide it
  • Marketing preferences — your opt-in/opt-out choice for promotional communications

2.2 Fridge & Inventory Data

When you use the app, we store:

  • Food item names, categories, quantities, and expiration dates
  • Barcode scan data and product lookups
  • Grocery list items
  • Consumption history (items marked as "used" or "trashed") for AI-powered insights
  • Shared fridge memberships and invite codes

2.3 Camera & Photos

When you use the camera scanner, photos are:

  • Processed temporarily to identify food items via Google's Gemini AI
  • Not stored permanently on our servers — images are discarded after analysis
  • Never shared with third parties for advertising purposes

2.4 Device & Usage Data

We may automatically collect:

  • Device type, operating system version
  • App crash reports and performance data
  • General usage patterns (e.g., which features you use most)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Core functionality: Managing your fridge inventory, grocery lists, and generating AI recipes
  • AI improvements: Consumption patterns help our AI suggest smarter expiration estimates and recipes
  • Notifications: Sending expiration alerts (only if you opt in)
  • Marketing: Promotional emails/texts (only if you opted in during signup; you can opt out anytime)
  • Support: Responding to your help requests and bug reports

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We share data only with:

  • Supabase — our database provider (hosts your account and inventory data with encryption at rest)
  • Google Gemini AI — processes camera images for food recognition (images are not retained)
  • Open Food Facts — barcode lookups to identify scanned products (no personal data sent)
  • Shared fridge members — users you invite to your fridge can see shared inventory and grocery lists

5. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Database access is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — users can only access their own data
  • API keys are stored server-side and never exposed in the client application
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained as long as your account exists
  • Consumed/trashed items: Kept for AI learning purposes but can be purged on request
  • Deleted accounts: All data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion (cascade delete)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — view all your information in the app
  • Correct your data — edit your profile in Settings
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account (permanently removes all data)
  • Opt out of marketing — uncheck marketing preferences in your profile
  • Export your data — contact us at privacy@smartfridge.ai

8. Children's Privacy

Smart Fridge AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data:

  • Email: privacy@smartfridge.ai
  • In-app: Settings → Contact Support
